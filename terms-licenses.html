Software purchase information

Software licence terms & conditions.

Understand the product-specific details that matter before you request or purchase a software licence.

Before purchase

Confirm the exact edition, licence channel, activation method, user/device scope and regional eligibility on your quote.

Browse licences

1. Product identity and licence source

The product name shown in the MGS catalogue is a guide to the item being offered. The exact Microsoft SKU, channel (for example OEM, retail or volume), edition, included applications, delivery format and licence rights are determined by the product specifically quoted and supplied. Where a catalogue shorthand does not uniquely identify an official SKU, MGS must confirm the product identity before accepting the order.

2. Licence duration and activation

Some Office and Windows products are offered under perpetual, one-time-purchase licence terms. A perpetual licence does not mean every product is transferable or usable on unlimited devices. Activation, user/device allocation, account binding, reinstallation and transfer rights are governed by the exact licence terms and applicable Microsoft product terms. Do not assume a “lifetime for one user” entitlement from the title alone; verify the terms for your SKU before purchase.

3. OEM and physical-media items

OEM licences can be subject to device-specific restrictions. DVD, USB, boxed and email labels describe the catalogue or delivery format and do not, by themselves, establish the licence rights. Check the product description and written quote for the exact contents and activation process.

4. Product compatibility

Customers are responsible for checking operating-system requirements and hardware compatibility before installation. For storage products, verify the supported form factor and interface. A 2.5-inch SATA SSD is not the same interface as an M.2 NVMe drive, and an M.2 connector alone does not guarantee NVMe compatibility.

5. Office 2021 support notice

Microsoft lists Office 2021 support ending on 13 October 2026. Microsoft states that after end of support it will no longer provide technical support, bug fixes or security fixes for Office 2021. Customers should consider this lifecycle information before requesting an Office 2021 product.

6. Pricing, tax and order acceptance

Prices are quoted in the currency shown in the offer and must be confirmed by MGS before purchase. Currency display does not itself fix an exchange rate, tax treatment, delivery charge or final payable amount. An enquiry is not an accepted order. The final written offer should set out product identity, quantities, final price, applicable taxes, delivery method and payment arrangements.

7. Delivery and digital products

Delivery method depends on the exact SKU. Digital keys, account-bound licences, email delivery and physical items can have different fulfilment requirements. MGS should provide fulfilment instructions and any restrictions in writing before order confirmation.

8. Refunds, returns and support

Return, cancellation, refund and after-sales support eligibility depends on the product type, applicable law and the written order terms. Digital licence keys and activation-sensitive products may be subject to special handling; no blanket no-refund or guaranteed-refund promise is made by this catalogue page. Contact MGS before purchase if you need the applicable policy.

9. Manufacturer terms

Microsoft product terms, activation rules and any applicable end-user licence agreement govern the rights associated with the Microsoft product. This summary is for product-selection information and does not replace the manufacturer’s terms.

10. Questions before ordering

For confirmation of exact SKU, supported operating systems, activation scope, app bundle, transfer eligibility or delivery format, contact MGS and ask for those details to be included in the written quote.

Important: This page is a customer-facing product-information summary, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. The seller’s registered legal entity, address, governing law, tax registration and final consumer/commercial sale conditions must be added to the formal terms before taking live orders.
